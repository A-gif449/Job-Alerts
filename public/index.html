A few things worth knowing

      

        
Will I get the same listing twice?
No. Every job we send you is recorded, so a listing that already reached your inbox is never mailed again — even after you change your keywords.

        
Can I change my role or location later?
Yes. Post the subscription card again while signed in and the new details overwrite your old preferences instead of creating a second one.

        
What happens if I unsubscribe?
Your preferences and send history are deleted immediately. If you subscribe again later, matching starts fresh.

        
Where do the listings come from?
A public job board API, checked on your plan's schedule — every 15 minutes on Express Mail, once a day at 9am on Surface Mail.
